   Rationale: Forked lacks any healing; ties to speed identity.

2. Mined - "Ore Restoration" [SUP]  
   [2 AP] — Heal ally 20% MaxHP; +5% per debuff removed from ally
   Rationale: Mined lacks support; ties to extraction theme.

3. Bot - "Self-Repair Protocol" [UTIL]
   [1 AP] — Heal 15% MaxHP at end of turn if no damage dealt this turn
   Rationale: Bot lacks self-sustain; fits automation patience theme.

4. Glitched - "Error Correction" [SUP]
   [2 AP] — Heal ally 15% MaxHP; 50% chance to remove or apply random debuff
   Rationale: Glitched lacks support; maintains chaos theme.

5. Meme - "Meme Magic" [SUP]
   [2 AP] — 50% heal ally 30% MaxHP; 50% heal self 15% MaxHP
   Rationale: Meme lacks support; high variance fits identity.
