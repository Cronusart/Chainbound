0. Purpose
Defines:
- Status classes and timing
- Authoritative status catalog
- Source rules (ChemistryOnly vs General)

1. Status Classes (Exclusive)
DurationStatus: persists N turns; reapply refreshes up to max
InstantStatus: resolves immediately
CounterStatus: increments counter; triggers at threshold
ConsumedStatus: persists until consumed OR expires

2. Duration Semantics (Authoritative)
All "xN" values represent duration in turns unless explicitly a CounterStatus increment.
No hidden intensity scaling unless explicitly stated in that status definition.

3. Turn Timing (Authoritative)
Start of Turn:
- Turn-start triggers
- Gain baseline AP (+2)
Action Resolution:
- hit check
- damage
- apply statuses (from inscriptions, passives, chemistry resolver)
End of Turn:
- DoT / regen
- status AP changes (Pumped/Drained etc)
- decrement durations

4. Cleanse & Immunity
- Cleanse removes DurationStatus debuffs (General only by default)
- Cleanse does not remove CounterStatus unless allowed
- Immunity prevents application; does not remove existing effects

5. Status Origin Types (Authoritative)
ChemistryOnly: can only be produced by chemistry resolver on damaging hit
General: can be applied by inscriptions/passives if they say so

7. Triggered Event Percentages (Authoritative Defaults)

When inscriptions or passives reference chance-based triggers, use these baseline values unless explicitly overridden:

7.1 Action Disruption
- Rekt (lose action): 25%
- Glitched Mind (mis-act): 50%
- Offline (wake on damage): 100% on any damage

7.2 AP-Related Triggers
- Refund AP on miss: 25%
- Bonus AP on crit: 25%
- AP steal per turn (MEV Extracted): 1 AP (100%)

7.3 Random Selection
- Random stat selection (Meme Surge, etc.): Equal probability across valid stats
- Random debuff selection: Equal probability across curated set

7.4 Conditional Triggers
- Default "on hit" trigger chance: 25%
- Default "on crit" trigger chance: 50%
- Default "end of turn" trigger chance: 100% (deterministic)

7.5 Recoil & Self-Damage
- Overleveraged recoil: 25% of damage dealt
- Overclocked recoil (legacy serialized reaction only): 8% MaxHP
- FOMO self-DoT: 1/16 MaxHP per turn

## 7.6 Chemistry Reaction Redesign v2

ChemistryOnly reactions use the seeded combat resolver and are recorded in the
forensic log. Each matrix cell has one primary output with an explicit Self or
Foe recipient. Chemistry reactions do not use stacks.

- Firewall: Duration 2. The first Projectile hit each turn deals 30% less damage;
  Contact hits are unaffected. Chemistry application still resolves after damage
  mitigation.
- Slowed: Duration 2. Speed -2 only. It does not roll an action skip.
- Shattered: Instant. Deal 8% of current HP (minimum 1) and apply Def -1; it is
  not an execute.
- Drip: Duration 3. Heal 6% MaxHP at each End of Turn for 3 ticks, including the turn it is applied.
- Leaked: Duration 3. While an active Chainling on that side has Leaked, each Chainling on that side that acts loses 10% of its own MaxHP (minimum 1). It is not an end-of-turn damage tick, and it does not hit the other side.
- Rugged: Duration 3. End-of-Turn damage is counter/16 of MaxHP, increasing by
  one per tick and capping at counter 15.
- FOMO: Duration 3. Atk +1 and 1/16 MaxHP self-damage at End of Turn.
- Anchored: Consumed. Suppresses spread expansion on the affected Chainling's
  next damaging move, including Broadcast and Splinter.
- Splinter: Consumed. The next damaging move hits each active foe at 65% damage
  when two or more foes are active; against one foe it deals 15% more final
  damage.
- Cascade Failure: Counter threshold 3. The third chemistry application deals
  25% MaxHP damage and resets the counter. Swapping out resets it; bench time
  does not advance it.
- Isolated: Ally-targeted Support effects, including healing, AP transfer, and
  stage buffs, are rejected. Self-support, field effects, damage, and swapping
  remain legal.

Firewalled, Halted, Crashed, LiquidityPump, Evasive, Bridged, Narrowcast, and Overclocked are
deprecated authoring IDs retained only for migration and serialized-save reads.

8. Resistance & Mitigation Rules

8.1 Single-Source Mitigation Cap
- Maximum damage mitigation from any single passive or effect: 30%
- Rationale: 50% mitigation is too strong for competitive balance

8.2 Mitigation Stacking
- Multiple mitigation sources stack multiplicatively, not additively
- Example: Two 20% sources = 1 - (0.8 * 0.8) = 36% total, not 40%
- Hard floor: Target always takes at least 10% of original damage

8.3 Resistance Passives
- Type-resistance passives: 20-25% mitigation (Rare tier)
- Conditional resistance passives: Up to 30% mitigation (Epic+ tier)
- No passive may grant full immunity to a damage type

10. New General Statuses (v1 Revisions)

The following statuses were added to support barrier bypass and damage redirect:

10.1 Unsealed (StatusId 307)
- Origin: General
- Class: Duration (2 turns)
- Effect: 50% chance per hit to bypass single-hit barriers
- Not a debuff or buff; applied via support moves
- Does NOT consume barrier hits when bypass triggers

10.2 Relay (StatusId 308)
- Origin: General
- Class: Consumed (1 stack)
- Effect: Full barrier bypass for the next hit
- Consumed on use (whether or not target had a barrier)
- Higher priority than Unsealed in bypass resolution

10.3 DamageIntercept (StatusId 309)
- Origin: General
- Class: Consumed (1 stack)
- Effect: Redirect next single-target attack on this Chainling to the interceptor
- Applied by damage_intercept and signal_redirect moves
- Sets redirectInterceptor reference on the protected Chainling
- Consumed when redirect triggers; reference cleared

All three are cataloged in Statuses.csv. See Data/_LEGEND.md for CSV format.
