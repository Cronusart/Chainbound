COMBAT_01_TypeChemistry_v2.0

0. Purpose
Defines Chainbound's authoritative, stackless type-chemistry behavior. Chemistry is deterministic, PvP-safe, data-driven, and fully loggable.

1. Canonical Types
Node, Gas, Block, Encrypted, Burned, Frozen, Forked, Mined, Staked, Compressed, Bot, Glitched, Shard, Meme

2. Resolution
Chemistry resolves only when a damaging hit lands.

Inputs:
- The hit's resolved attack type.
- The defender's resolved type or types, including any active coating override.
- The attacker-row / defender-column interaction matrix.

For each defender type, a forward matrix edge contributes +1 and a reverse edge contributes -1. The summed score collapses to one relation:
- Positive: Advantage, flat x1.2 damage.
- Zero: Neutral, flat x1.0 damage.
- Negative: Resisted, flat x0.8 damage.

Dual typing never compounds damage beyond these three values. If multiple forward cells qualify, the defender's primary type selects the reaction first. The relation is evaluated on every hit, but a foe-targeted reaction applies at most once to each target per action and a self-targeted reaction applies at most once to the attacker per action.

3. Matrix Authoring
Rows are attacker types and columns are defender types. A cell is either '-' or exactly one reaction using:

Status Name @Self
Status Name @Foe

Counts such as x1/x2 and compound outputs joined by '+' are invalid. @Self means the attacking Chainling receives the reaction; @Foe means the defending Chainling receives it.

4. Chemistry Families
All newly authored reactions belong to one of eight tactical families:
- Liquidity: Pumped, Drained.
- Volatility: Rugged, FOMO.
- Latency: Jammed, Slowed, Isolated.
- Structure: Firewall, Shattered, Corroded, Dusted, Reinforced, Anchored.
- Exposure: Backlash Type, Leaked.
- Sustain: Siphoned, Drip.
- Cleanse: Defueled.
- Contagion: Cascade Failure, Splinter.

Beneficial and hybrid reactions target @Self. Harmful reactions target @Foe. This polarity is validated during import.

5. Stateful Reactions
Chemistry is stackless except for two explicitly named state values:
- Cascade Failure exposes a 0/3 trigger counter. The third application deals 25% MaxHP damage and resets; swapping out resets it, and bench time pauses it.
- Rugged exposes severity 1-15. It deals severity/16 MaxHP damage at End of Turn, then increases severity by one. Reapplication refreshes duration without increasing severity.

6. Determinism and Logging
All chemistry proc rolls use CombatRng. Forensic output records the relation, selected reaction, recipient, application or block result, roll/chance/branch where applicable, counter transitions, and swap-triggered Cascade resets.

7. Compatibility
Firewalled, Halted, Crashed, LiquidityPump, Evasive, Bridged, Narrowcast, Overclocked, TypeCoating, HypedTypeCoating, and Amplified remain readable where needed for migration or serialized assets but cannot be newly authored into the v1 interaction matrix.

8. Authority Boundary
Chemistry resolution is local deterministic combat authority. MagicBlock VRF, Solana instructions, and NFT minting do not participate in chemistry resolution.

END
