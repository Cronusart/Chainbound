COMBAT_03A_Moves_CoreFramework_v1.1

0. Purpose & Scope
Defines move structure, slot rules, equip rules, and constraints.
Excludes: numeric tuning, modifiers, final move lists.

1. Slot System (Locked)
SUPERSEDED: See MOVE_LOADOUT_RULES_AND_UI_SPEC.md.
Current runtime uses rebirth-based capacity: 4 + rebirthCount, clamped to 4-9.
Stars and evolution stage do not increase move slots.
Slot-consuming modifiers are defined in COMBAT_05_MoveModifiers_v1.1.txt; capped by rules.

2. Categories
Damaging (Melee/Ranged)
Status/Utility
Support (subset of Status/Utility)
Passive

3. Equip Eligibility (Authoritative)
A Chainling may equip from:
(A) its type pool(s),
(B) its species-specific pool,
(C) typeless pool.

4. Typeless vs Typed
4.1 Typed damaging moves:
- have explicit type
- trigger chemistry on hit via chemistry resolver

4.2 Typeless moves:
- Non-damaging typeless moves do not trigger chemistry.
- Typeless DAMAGING moves resolve their type at runtime:
  AttackTypeResolved = user’s current type(s) (or a defined selection rule if dual-type).
  They trigger chemistry exactly like typed damaging moves.

5. Chemistry-Only Outputs (Hard Constraint)
Any status/effect marked ChemistryOnly in the Status Catalog:
- may ONLY be produced by the chemistry resolver on damaging hit
- must NOT be directly applied by moves or passives

6. Intrinsic Properties (Immutable)
Moves have fixed:
- Power
- AP Cost
- Accuracy
- Targeting
- Base type (if typed)

Rarity affects availability/complexity only, not raw scaling.

6.1 Rarity Semantics (Clarified)
Rarity tiers indicate complexity and availability, NOT power:

Common: Single-purpose, straightforward execution
- Example: "Deal damage" or "Buff self +1 stage"

Uncommon: Reliable or slightly conditional
- Example: "Deal damage; 25% chance to lower target's Def by 1 stage"

Rare: Conditional or dual-purpose
- Example: "If target is debuffed, deal bonus damage"

Epic: Multi-condition or stat substitution
- Example: "If HP < 50%, gain +2 stages and heal; otherwise, deal damage"

Legendary: Rule exceptions with constraints
- Example: "Ignore type chemistry disadvantage for this hit"

Mythic: Unique behaviors with meaningful downsides
- Example: "Deal massive damage but skip your next turn"

Key principle: A Common move at max evolution should remain competitive with Epic moves in raw output. Epic moves offer flexibility, not superiority.

7. Logging Requirement
All moves and passive triggers must be deterministic and fully loggable.

9. Wall/Barrier Interactions

9.1 Barrier Mechanics
Barriers and guard effects are General statuses that provide damage mitigation:
- Barriers have a duration OR a hit/damage threshold
- Multi-hit attacks are effective against barriers (each hit counts)
- See COMBAT_02C Section 6 for multi-hit resolution rules

9.2 Barrier Categories
- Single-hit barriers: Block next incoming hit entirely, then expire
  (hitsRemaining = N; each incoming hit consumes 1)
- Damage-threshold barriers: Absorb X damage before breaking
  (damageThreshold = X; incoming damage reduces remaining capacity)
- Duration barriers: Reduce damage by % for N turns
  (handled by existing MitigationSource system)

9.3 Wall Move Design
Support moves that create barriers should:
- Specify barrier type (single-hit, threshold, duration)
- Have clear break conditions logged
- Be counterable by multi-hit strategies

9.4 Barrier Bypass (v1 Revisions)
Three bypass sources exist, checked in this priority order:
1. **Relay** (General ConsumedStatus): Full bypass for next hit; consumed on use
2. **Unsealed** (General DurationStatus, 2 turns): 50% chance per hit to bypass
   single-hit barriers
3. **Modifier 4.3-b** (move modifier): First hit of the move bypasses barriers

When bypass triggers, the barrier is NOT consumed — the hit passes through cleanly.

9.5 Barrier Reflection
Barriers may define a reflectPercent (0-100). When a barrier absorbs damage,
the attacker receives floor(absorbed * reflectPercent / 100) as typeless recoil.

10. Support Move Families (v1 Revisions)

10.1 AP Transfer Moves
Tag: APMAN | SUP. Transfer AP between allies.
- ap_donation: Transfer 2 AP from self to partner; once per turn per user
- ap_redistribution: Move up to 2 AP from one ally to another
- ap_router (Node): Transfer up to 2 AP between any two allies
Restriction: apDonationUsedThisTurn flag prevents multiple donations per turn.

10.2 Damage Redirect Moves
Tag: SUP. Redirect incoming attacks to self.
- damage_intercept: Redirect next single-target attack on ally to self
  (applies DamageIntercept consumed status + sets redirectInterceptor reference)
- signal_redirect (Node): Redirect next hit on ally to self; +1 Def stage
Resolution: When a single-target attack hits a target with DamageIntercept,
damage is redirected to the interceptor instead. DamageIntercept is consumed.

10.3 Buff/Debuff Transfer Moves
Tag: SUP. Move status effects between Chainlings.
- effect_relay: Move 1 General debuff from ally to self; cleanse if none found
- relay_packet (Node): Move 1 General buff or debuff between allies
Only General-origin, non-Consumed statuses are eligible for transfer.

10.4 Position Swap
Tag: UTIL | SUP | PRIOR. Swap field positions with partner.
- position_swap: Swap active slot indices; does not trigger exit/entry effects
  Priority tag ensures it resolves before normal combat moves.
  Slot-based targeting persists after swap (enemies still target the slot, not the
  Chainling that was there).

11. Healing Moves (v1 Revisions)

11.1 Percentage-Based Healing
Healing moves may define healPercentMaxHP (integer 0-100):
- When non-zero, heal amount = floor(target.EffectiveMaxHP * healPercentMaxHP / 100)
- Minimum heal amount: 1 HP
- Falls back to flat basePower healing if healPercentMaxHP is 0

11.2 Terminology
- "Partner" = the other active ally on the field (2v2 format)
- "Team" = self + partner + bench (all owned Chainlings)
See _LEGEND.md Ally Terminology section for full definitions.

12. Type Move Design Guidelines
(See COMBAT_TypeProfiles.md for full playstyle context)

When generating or extending type pools, moves should primarily reinforce
the type's listed keywords. Off-theme moves (1-2 max per typed pool) are
acceptable for flexible builds but must not redefine the type's identity.

Node    — SUP majority; APMAN; AOE buff/heal; redirect. Avoid: direct debuff.
Gas     — APMAN; volatile self-trade; Bridging setup. Avoid: pure defense.
Block   — Barriers; defense ramp; grounding/slow. Avoid: speed or burst moves.
Encrypted — AP drain; debuff immunity; cipher effects. Avoid: direct DoT.
Burned  — DoT; burn coatings; atk debuffs; rug mechanics. Avoid: healing/sustain.
Frozen  — Speed debuffs; Jam; asset lock; stall. Avoid: high-damage burst.
Forked  — AoE DMG; chain split; speed burst; recoil tradeoff. Avoid: sustain.
Mined   — Physical ramp; conditional damage; FOMO scaling. Avoid: team support.
Staked  — Siphon; evasion; leech; patience passives. Avoid: aggressive burst.
Compressed — Precision; Jam; evasion; AP efficiency. Avoid: AoE.
Bot     — Multi-thread DMG; automated debuffs; AP denial. Avoid: healing/setup.
Glitched — Random/chaotic; exploit setups; def corruption. Avoid: reliable buffs.
Shard   — Single-target precision; partition/field effects. Avoid: AoE spam.
Meme    — High-risk/high-reward; random outcomes; all-in. Avoid: consistent reliable effects.
