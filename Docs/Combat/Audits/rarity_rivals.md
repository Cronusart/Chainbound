# Rarity rivals after audit reconciliation v1

Date: 2026-10-19

This is the post-reconciliation review list. “Rival” means a lower-rarity move in the same practical lane can match or beat the higher-rarity card after power, accuracy, hit count, AP cost, and the implemented rider are considered.

| Pair / card | Result | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Cipher Burst vs Cipher Shot | Cipher Burst is a Common multi-hit attack; Cipher Shot is Uncommon and guarantees two turns of Cipher Locked on its first landed hit. The status rider keeps the roles distinct. | Accepted because Cipher Shot buys reliable control rather than raw multi-hit output. |
| Deep Dig vs Ore Strike | Deep Dig is an Uncommon 72-power, 3 AP heavy hit; Ore Strike is a Rare 52-power, 2 AP contact hit. The rarity/cost ordering remains awkward. | Fix in V3. |
| Pickaxe Flurry vs Refined Shot | Pickaxe Flurry's multi-hit body and capped self-Atk gain can outvalue the cleaner ranged hit. | Fix in V3 after multi-hit expected value is reviewed. |
| Hype Train | Legendary, 52 power, 3 AP, no rider. | Fix in V3; riderless high rarity. |
| Combustive Shift | Legendary, two 22-power hits, 3 AP, no rider. | Fix in V3; riderless high rarity. |
| Bulwark Blow | Epic, 50 power, 3 AP, attacks with Melee Def. | Accepted because Def substitution enables a tank-only damage lane that ordinary attacks cannot copy. |
| System Reset | Common, 3 AP, strips all General buffs and debuffs, recharge 2. | Accepted non-rival: it is symmetric control with a skipped-action constraint, not a damage competitor. |
| Hype Burst | Common, 2 AP, removes up to two buffs and gains Atk only for buffs actually removed. | Accepted non-rival: conditional anti-buff utility does not obsolete an Uncommon damage/support card. |
| Pulse Shot | Common typeless, Power 26, Accuracy 95, 2 AP. | Accepted. It remains in the budget-clean typeless band and creates no new rival. |

The written band is: typeless single-target Common at 2 AP uses Power 26 / Accuracy 95 for budget-clean attacks and Power 30 for premium-clean or accuracy/risk variants. Typed Commons target roughly index 34 at 2 AP. Higher rarity must win through output or an implemented rider, not tooltip-only behavior.
