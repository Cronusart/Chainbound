## 1. Who We Are

Chainbound is operated by **SunOrc Games**, a game studio based in Texas, USA. We are the "controller" of the personal information described in this policy (meaning we decide how and why it is used).

Contact for privacy questions and requests: **chainboundgame@gmail.com**

## 2. Information We Collect

What we collect depends on how you play. Here's a summary:

| Category | Examples | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| **Guest and device identifiers** | A guest ID generated for your installation, device or app identifiers, device type, operating system, app version | Your device, automatically |
| **Platform / Seeker identity** | An identifier from your Solana Seeker platform identity, or from platform sign-in (such as Google Play Games or Sign in with Apple) **where available** | The platform, when you choose to sign in |
| **Account data** | Account ID, sign-in and authentication data, linked identities, and linking/restore information, stored with our backend provider Supabase | You and the platforms you sign in with |
| **Wallet data** | Your wallet's **public address**, and the **signed challenge message** used to verify you own the wallet | Your wallet, when you choose to connect it |
| **Gameplay and progress data** | Player name (if any), Chainlings and items, party, RAM balance and daily RAM earned, wins and losses, vending roll counters, in-game position, settings, and other game progress | Your gameplay |
| **Purchase and transaction records** | RAM shop purchases, mints, and other in-game Solana transactions, including transaction signatures, amounts, timestamps, and related wallet addresses | Your wallet transactions and the Solana blockchain |
| **PvP / multiplayer network data** | IP address, device and network information, session and match identifiers, needed to connect you to matches | Your device, processed through Unity Gaming Services |
| **Support communications** | Your email address, messages, and anything you choose to include (for example, screenshots or transaction signatures) | You, when you email us |
| **Security and fair-play data** | Logs and signals used to detect cheating, fraud, abuse, and errors | Your device and our systems |

**About the wallet signature.** To verify wallet ownership, you sign a challenge message. This message is **not a transaction**, does not move any funds, and does not give us access to your wallet.

**About IP addresses.** Our service providers (such as Supabase and Unity) may receive your IP address when your device connects to them, as part of how internet services work.

## 3. Information We Do Not Collect

- **We do not collect payment card or bank details.** Purchases are paid in SOL through your own self-custodial wallet.
- We do not ask for your real name, home address, or government ID to play.
- We do not currently use third-party analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs (see [Section 8](#8-analytics-advertising-and-tracking)).

## 4. On-Chain Data: Public and Permanent

If you connect a wallet, a **player profile account** tied to your wallet address may be created on the Solana blockchain. It may store data such as:

- wins and losses,
- your active party Chainling IDs,
- your RAM balance and daily RAM earned,
- vending roll counters,
- records of Random Rewards (such as Candy Machine rolls), which hold hashed identifiers, the random value, the odds used, and a hash of the result, and
- your last known in-game position.

In addition, your RAM purchases, mints, NFT ownership, and other Solana transactions are recorded on-chain together with your wallet address.

**Please understand:**

- **It's public.** Anyone can view on-chain data using blockchain explorers and other tools.
- **It's permanent.** Data written to a blockchain generally cannot be removed.
- **We can't edit or delete it.** Your privacy rights to delete or correct data (Section 11) **apply only to off-chain data we hold**, not to data on the blockchain.
- **It may be linked to you.** A wallet address is pseudonymous, but it can be linked to your identity if you, or others, connect it to information about you elsewhere.

If you don't want gameplay data on a public blockchain, you can play as a guest without connecting a wallet.

## 5. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- **Run the Game:** create and maintain your guest profile or account, save and restore progress, link accounts and wallets, and provide game features.
- **Process purchases and transactions:** deliver RAM and items, process mints, confirm transactions, and resolve purchase issues.
- **Power multiplayer:** match you with other players and connect PvP battles.
- **Keep things fair and safe:** detect and prevent cheating, exploits, bots, fraud, abuse, and security incidents; enforce our Terms.
- **Verify eligibility:** apply our 18+ age requirement and sanctions or legal restrictions.
- **Support you:** answer your emails and requests.
- **Improve and fix the Game:** debug errors and improve features, using our own server data and logs.
- **Communicate with you** about the Game, such as important changes to our terms or this policy.
- **Comply with law:** meet legal obligations, respond to lawful requests, and protect our rights.

We do **not** use your information for targeted advertising, and we do **not** sell it.

## 7. How We Share Information

We share information only as described here:

- **Service providers (processors):**
  - **Supabase** — our backend provider for database, authentication, and serverless functions.
  - **Unity Gaming Services** — multiplayer, relay, and lobby services for PvP, which process network data such as IP address and device information to connect matches.

  They process information on our behalf and under their own security and privacy commitments.
- **The Solana network:** when you transact or your on-chain profile is updated, data is broadcast to the public Solana blockchain and to the RPC providers, validators, and wallets that relay it. That data is public by nature.
- **MagicBlock (verifiable randomness):** when a roll is requested, MagicBlock's oracle reads the public on-chain roll request and writes back a random value. It receives only what is already on-chain; we do not send it your name, email, or other off-chain information.
- **Wallets and marketplaces:** your wallet provider, and any marketplace you use to trade NFTs, receive information directly from you under their own privacy policies.
- **The Solana dApp Store and platforms:** the dApp Store and platform sign-in providers may receive information related to your download, installation, or sign-in under their own policies.
- **Other players:** your player name, battle results, and similar game information may be visible to other players.
- **Legal requirements and safety:** when we believe in good faith it's required by law, legal process, or government request, or needed to protect the rights, property, or safety of players, us, or others, or to enforce our Terms.
- **Business transfers:** if we're involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that deal, subject to this policy or one at least as protective.
- **With your consent:** when you ask us to or agree to it.

**We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral (targeted) advertising.**

## 9. How Long We Keep Information

Our approach is simple: **your player account information is stored on our backend (Supabase) while your account is active.** We do not keep personal information outside of that unless we need it for a specific purpose below, and temporary data is automatically deleted on a rolling basis.

| Data | How long |
|---|---|
| Account, guest, and progress data | While your account or guest profile is active. **Deleted within 30 days** after you request deletion or after your account is closed, and removed from backups within a further 30 days as backups roll off. Beta resets may delete it sooner. |
| Purchase and transaction records (off-chain) | While your account is active. After deletion or closure, we keep these records only **as long as required by applicable law** (for example, for tax and accounting purposes), and then delete them. |
| Wallet verification records | While the wallet is linked. Deleted within 30 days after you unlink the wallet or your account is deleted or closed. |
| Multiplayer network data | Only as long as needed to run matches and for security, according to Unity's and our retention settings |
| Support emails | Deleted **within 90 days after your request is resolved** |
| Security and anti-cheat logs, and other temporary data | **Automatically deleted on a rolling basis within 30 days**, unless we need to keep specific records longer to investigate a particular security incident, cheating, fraud, or abuse, or to enforce our Terms |
| **On-chain data** | **Permanent.** We cannot delete it. |

We may keep specific information longer only where required by law, or where needed to resolve a pending dispute or enforce our agreements, and only for as long as that need lasts. We may also keep information that has been de-identified so it no longer identifies you.

## 10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, including access controls on our backend and relying on established providers. Wallet ownership is verified by signature, so we never need your private keys.

No system is 100% secure. Please protect your device, sign-in credentials, and wallet seed phrase, and never share your seed phrase with anyone. If we learn of a security breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you and regulators as required by law.

## 11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the rights below. We aim to honor reasonable requests from all players, wherever they live.

**Rights for off-chain data we hold:**

- **Access / know:** ask what personal information we have about you and get a copy.
- **Correction:** ask us to fix inaccurate information.
- **Deletion:** ask us to delete your personal information. *(This does **not** apply to on-chain data, which we cannot delete. It also may not apply to data we must keep for legal reasons.)*
- **Portability / export:** get your data in a commonly used, machine-readable format.
- **Opt-out:** opt out of the sale of personal information, targeted advertising, or certain profiling. *(We don't do any of these, but you may still contact us.)*
- **Withdraw consent:** where we rely on consent, withdraw it at any time.
- **Non-discrimination:** we won't treat you differently for exercising your rights.
- **Appeal:** if we deny your request, you can appeal (see Section 12).

### Texas residents (Texas Data Privacy and Security Act)

You have the right to confirm whether we process your personal data, access it, correct inaccuracies, delete it, obtain a portable copy, and opt out of sale, targeted advertising, and certain profiling. We do not sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising. If we decline your request, you may **appeal** by replying to our decision email with the subject "Privacy Appeal." If your appeal is denied, you may contact the **Texas Attorney General** (https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/consumer-protection).

### California residents (CCPA / CPRA)

- **Categories collected in the last 12 months:** identifiers (guest ID, account ID, wallet address, IP address, email if you contact us); commercial information (purchase and transaction records); internet or network activity (gameplay data, multiplayer connection data); and inferences limited to fair-play and security signals. See Section 2 for sources and Section 5 for purposes.
- **Disclosure:** we disclose these categories to service providers (Supabase and Unity) for business purposes described in Section 7.
- **No sale or sharing:** we do **not sell** or **share** (for cross-context behavioral advertising) personal information, and have not done so in the last 12 months. We do not knowingly sell or share data of consumers under 16.
- **Sensitive personal information:** we do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes that require a right to limit under the CPRA.
- **Your rights:** to know, delete, correct, and not be discriminated against. You may use an **authorized agent**, and we may ask for proof of their authority and verify your identity.
- **Retention:** see Section 9.

### EEA and UK residents (GDPR / UK GDPR)

You have the rights listed above, plus the right to **object** to processing based on legitimate interests and to **restrict** processing in certain cases. You also have the right to **lodge a complaint** with your local data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office). [[EU/UK REPRESENTATIVE, if required]]

## 12. How to Make a Request

1. **Email** **chainboundgame@gmail.com** with the subject line **"Privacy Request"**.
2. **Tell us** what you want (access, correction, deletion, export, opt-out, or appeal) and where you live.
3. **Help us find you:** include your guest ID or account ID (found in [[IN-GAME LOCATION OF PLAYER/ACCOUNT ID, e.g. Settings > Account]]) and/or your linked wallet address.
4. **Verification:** we may ask you to verify your identity, for example by signing a message with your linked wallet or confirming details from your account. We'll only use verification information for that purpose.

**Response time:** we will respond within **30 days** of receiving your request, and may extend this where the law allows (we'll tell you if so).

**Appeals:** if we decline your request in whole or part, reply to our decision with the subject **"Privacy Appeal"** and we will respond within **60 days**.

## 13. Age Requirement (18+) and Children

- **Chainbound is for adults only.** You must be at least **18 years old** (or the age of majority where you live, if higher) to use the Game, including guest play. The Game is **not directed to anyone under 18**, and we do **not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18**.
- **Children under 13.** Because the Game is for adults only, it is also not directed to children under 13, and, consistent with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
- **If we learn a user is under 18,** we will close the account and delete the off-chain personal information we hold about that user, following the timelines in [Section 9](#9-how-long-we-keep-information). (We cannot delete data already written to the blockchain.)
- If you believe someone under 18 has used the Game or given us personal information, email **chainboundgame@gmail.com** with the subject **"Underage User."**

Where required (for example, for EEA or UK data), we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, or other lawful transfer mechanisms offered by our providers.
